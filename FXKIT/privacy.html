Privacy

Your files stay on your device.

FXKIT is a static website. Every effect runs in your browser using your own device’s processor. The images, videos and audio you open are never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing from the app itself. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no third-party trackers.

Like every website, the hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Vercel’s privacy policy.

Exports

When you export, the file is created in your browser and saved straight to your device.
